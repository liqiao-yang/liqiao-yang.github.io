About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Szeged, Hungary (since September 2022). My research primarily focuses on Applied Economics, particularly Regional Economics, Economic Geography, and International Trade, with an emphasis on industrial development and regional transformation. My research interests also extend to Industrial Economics and Marketing.
